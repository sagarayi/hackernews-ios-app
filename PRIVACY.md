# Privacy Policy — HN Reader

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

HN Reader is an unofficial Hacker News client. This policy explains what the app does with your information: effectively, nothing.

## Data Not Collected

HN Reader collects **no personal data of any kind**:

- No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles
- No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising identifiers
- No tracking of any kind, on-device or across apps and websites
- Nothing you read or tap is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere except as described below

## Network Requests

To show you stories, your device directly fetches public content from:

- The Firebase Hacker News API (story and comment data)
- news.ycombinator.com (story ordering)
- archive.today (archived copies of linked articles, on demand)

These are standard content requests containing no personal information. Each of those services operates under its own privacy policy. Links you choose to share leave the app through the iOS share sheet under your control.

## Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children under 13 either.
